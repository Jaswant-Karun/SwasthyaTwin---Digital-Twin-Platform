# Biomarker Selection Rationale & Physical Limits

## Selected Biomarker Panel
- **Fasting Plasma Glucose (FPG)**: Primary indicator of acute glycemic regulation.
- **HbA1c**: 90-day integrated indicator of erythrocyte glycation.
- **Systolic / Diastolic Blood Pressure**: Primary hemodynamics metrics assessing vascular tone and peripheral resistance.
- **Lipid Panel (Total Cholesterol, HDL, LDL, Triglycerides)**: Atherogenic index computation.
- **Serum Creatinine & eGFR**: Critical markers for early diabetic nephropathy screening.
