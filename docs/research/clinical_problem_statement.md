# Clinical Problem Statement & Project Charter

## 1. Executive Summary
Chronic metabolic and cardiovascular diseases represent over 70% of preventable global morbidity. Current clinical management operates reactively: patients are evaluated at 3-to-6 month intervals, leaving significant gaps during which subclinical deterioration accelerates unnoticed.

## 2. Core Value Proposition
SwasthyaTwin closes this feedback loop by establishing an individual-level Digital Twin that continuously integrates multi-sensor telemetry, runs predictive trajectory engines, and computes counterfactual scenario simulations to guide proactive lifestyle and clinical interventions.
