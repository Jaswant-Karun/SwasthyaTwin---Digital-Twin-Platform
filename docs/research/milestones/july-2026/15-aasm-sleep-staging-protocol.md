# 5-Stage AASM Sleep Classification Protocol (July 15, 2026)
## Sleep Stage Definitions
- **Wake**: High autonomic arousal and movement.
- **Stage N1**: Drowsiness, initial transition from wakefulness.
- **Stage N2**: Light stable sleep, dominant nocturnal proportion (~50%).
- **Stage N3**: Deep slow-wave sleep, autonomic nadir, physical restoration.
- **Stage REM**: Rapid eye movement, cortical activation, dreaming architecture.
