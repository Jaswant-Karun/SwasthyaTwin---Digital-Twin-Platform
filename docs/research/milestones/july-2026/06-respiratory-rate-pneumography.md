# Respiratory Rate & Impedance Pneumography Guidelines (July 6, 2026)
## Signal Extraction
Respiratory rate is captured through chest impedance pneumography or respiratory sinus arrhythmia (RSA) modulation.
- Canonical unit: `breaths/min`.
- Normal resting range: 12.0–20.0 breaths/min.
- Bounds: 6.0–60.0 breaths/min.
