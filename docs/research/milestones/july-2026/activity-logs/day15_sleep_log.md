### Markov Transition Probabilities
Initial state transition matrix calibrated across normal sleep.

### Wake Stage
Characterized by high frequency pulse oscillations and elevated LF/HF.

### Stage N1
Initial alpha attenuation with subtle slowing of median heart rate.

### Stage N2
Stable parasympathetic maintenance with regular respiratory coupling.

### Stage N3 Slow Wave
Marked by profound cardiovascular slowing (nadir) and high RMSSD.

### REM Sleep
Desynchronized phasic bursts of sympathetic activity resembling wakefulness.
