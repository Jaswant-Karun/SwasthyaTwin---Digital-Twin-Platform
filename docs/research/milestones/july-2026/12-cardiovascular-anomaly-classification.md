# Cardiovascular Statistical Deviation Classification (July 12, 2026)
## Classification Levels
- `normal`: Within corridor (score $\in [-2.0, 2.0]$).
- `elevated`: Statistical excursion above upper corridor (score $> 2.0$).
- `low`: Statistical excursion below lower corridor (score $< -2.0$).
- `insufficient_data`: Fewer than 5 valid baseline samples available.
