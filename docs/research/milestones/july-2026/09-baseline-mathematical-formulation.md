# Non-Parametric Median Baseline Mathematics (July 9, 2026)
## Rationale
Parametric estimators (mean, standard deviation) are easily distorted by acute transient outliers.
$$\text{Baseline Median} = \text{Median}(\{x_i\}_{i=1}^N)$$
Ensures robust central tendency tracking over 30-day observational horizons.
