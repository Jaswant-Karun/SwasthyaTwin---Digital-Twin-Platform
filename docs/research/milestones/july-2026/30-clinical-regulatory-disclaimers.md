# Regulatory Positioning & Non-Diagnostic Clinical Disclaimers (July 30, 2026)
## Safety & Ethics Framework
SwasthyaTwin AI is an investigational research and personal wellness intelligence platform.
- Statistical anomaly flags do not diagnose cardiovascular disease or arrhythmia.
- Machine learning sleep classifications are research estimates, not diagnostic polysomnography.
- Users experiencing adverse symptoms are instructed to consult licensed medical practitioners.
