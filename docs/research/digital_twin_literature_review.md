# Biophysical Digital Twins in Preventive Medicine: Literature Review

## Synthesis of Key Findings
1. **Model Complexity vs Latency Tradeoff**: Differential equation-based whole-organ models offer biological realism but suffer from prohibitive computational latency. Surrogate ML models provide millisecond inference suitable for interactive clinical interfaces.
2. **Hybrid Modeling**: Combining deterministic clinical guidelines (AHA/ACC, ADA) with probabilistic estimators prevents unphysical model hallucination while maintaining high predictive sensitivity.
