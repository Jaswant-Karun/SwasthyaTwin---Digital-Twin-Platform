# Clinical Biomarker Standards & Normalization

## Biomarker Specifications
| Biomarker | Standard Unit | Normal Range | Critical Threshold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Fasting Blood Glucose | mg/dL | 70 - 99 | < 50 or > 250 |
| HbA1c | % | 4.0 - 5.6 | > 8.5 |
| Systolic Blood Pressure | mmHg | 90 - 120 | > 180 |
| Diastolic Blood Pressure | mmHg | 60 - 80 | > 120 |
| Total Cholesterol | mg/dL | 125 - 200 | > 240 |
| Serum Creatinine | mg/dL | 0.7 - 1.3 | > 2.0 |
