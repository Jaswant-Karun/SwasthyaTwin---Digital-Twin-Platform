# Digital Twin Synchronization Pipeline

## 1. Lifecycle Phases
1. **Observation**: Raw telemetry packet arrival (vitals, lab diagnostics, lifestyle metrics).
2. **Validation & Cleansing**: Outlier rejection, physiological bounds check, missing data imputation.
3. **State Reconciliation**: Merging telemetry with historical baseline profile.
4. **Organ Health Scoring**: Re-evaluating metabolic, cardiovascular, renal, and pulmonary indices.
5. **Trajectory Projection**: Recalibrating 30-day, 90-day, and 1-year predictive vectors.
